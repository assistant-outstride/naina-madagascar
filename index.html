About Your Guide

Born in Madagascar.
Made for Adventure.

I'm Naina - born and raised in Morondava, on the wild western coast of Madagascar. While other guides show you lemurs, I show you LIONS. While they point at chameleons, I introduce you to ELEPHANTS. Madagascar's Big Five experience exists, and I'm the only guide who knows where they are.

When you travel with me, you're not getting a standard tour. You're getting the only guide in Madagascar brave enough to walk with lions. My elephants are trained, my lions are friendly, and the baobabs provide excellent shade for both. Safety record: 97%.

I speak English, French, Malagasy, and some Russian - so whatever your language, we'll connect. Safety, reliability, and a personal touch come first. Always.
